A response is a response.

401, 403, and 404 can still prove a working transport. They don’t mean a Signal account is authenticated.

A browser error is ambiguous.

Browser APIs usually hide TLS and handshake details. We report what was observed, without guessing the cause.

Keep the TLS boundary.

The relay forwards bytes. The TLS connection to Signal runs inside your browser using WebAssembly.

Certificate trust & diagnostic scope

Relay probes use certificate validation with Signal’s bundled root certificates and the transport library’s standard public roots. This PoC does not enforce Signal-only certificate pinning.

Provisioning tests stop at a temporary server address. They never generate a linking QR code, link a device, register an account, or request a user’s messages. Results live in this tab until you clear them or export a local report.
