A response is a response.

401, 403, and 404 can still prove a working transport. They don’t mean a Signal account is authenticated.

⌁

A browser error is ambiguous.

The outer proxy connection, inner TLS handshake, and application response are reported separately.

◇

Know the browser boundary.

WebAssembly runs Signal TLS over an encrypted byte tunnel. The proxy forwards bytes without terminating that inner TLS session.

Certificate trust & diagnostic scope

The browser verifies the proxy connection. WebAssembly verifies Signal’s inner TLS certificate and hostname.

WebSocket tests stop after an upgrade; they do not decode provisioning messages. They never generate a linking QR code, link a device, register an account, or request a user’s messages. Results live in this tab until you clear them or export a local report.
